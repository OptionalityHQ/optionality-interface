Optionality Protocol Documentation

Optionality is a no-slippage, zero-liquidation options protocol on NEAR. Every option is a defined-risk vertical spread executed at the pool's published quote and backed 100% by escrowed USDT in the on-chain LP Vault—eliminating order-book slippage, margin calls, liquidation cascades, and counterparty default risk.

Bounded Vertical Spreads Every Call and Put has a defined lower and upper strike `[L, H]`, capping worst-case liability at `Width × Contracts`. Exact Put-Call Parity Priced via analytical Black-Scholes with skew smile so `CallSpread(L,H) + PutSpread(L,H) = H - L`. Multi-Oracle Settlement 4 independent seats (Kraken, OKX, Pyth, Venue Index) require a quorum within 30 bps tolerance to fix a series.

1. Protocol Overview Architecture

Traditional crypto options venues suffer from two structural flaws: fragmented orderbooks across dozens of strikes/expiries, and under-collateralized margin engines that liquidate traders during volatility spikes.

Optionality solves both problems by combining a Single Counterparty LP Pool Vault with Bounded Vertical Spreads on NEAR Protocol (~1.0s finality):

  • For Buyers (Takers): Your maximum possible loss is strictly your upfront USDT entry cost. There are zero margin calls and zero liquidations regardless of how far spot moves before expiry.
  • For Liquidity Providers (LPs): Because every ticket's maximum payout is bounded by its strike width W = High - Low, the smart contract locks 100% of the worst-case payout inside reserved equity at the moment the ticket is written.
  • Dual Execution Path: Traders can buy from the LP Pool (or take a cheaper resting offer on the on-chain CLOB if a maker has posted a better price).

2. Bounded Vertical Spreads Payoff Structure

Instead of writing naked options with unbounded downside, every instrument in Optionality is a vertical spread defined by a lower strike L (low_strike) and an upper strike H (high_strike), with width W = H - L:

  • Standard Widths: $100 on ETH spreads and $4,000 on BTC spreads.
  • Call Spread (Bullish): Struck at Strike = L, capping at H = L + W. Profits as the underlying rises above L + UnitCost, reaching maximum payout W per contract at or above H.
  • Put Spread (Bearish): Struck at Strike = H, capping at L = H - W. Profits as the underlying falls below H - UnitCost, reaching maximum payout W per contract at or below L.
// Payoff per contract at expiry settlement price S_T:
CallSpreadPayoff(S_T) = clamp(S_T - L, 0, H - L)
PutSpreadPayoff(S_T)  = clamp(H - S_T, 0, H - L)

// Net P&L for N contracts (fractional or whole):
NetPnL = N * PayoffPerContract(S_T) - TotalEntryCost

3. The 100% Solvency & Custody Invariant On-Chain Accounting

The `bounded_options` contract enforces a strict conservation law across all ten internal token buckets on every state transition. At any block height, `expected_custody()` equals the contract's actual NEP-141 USDT token balance (`ft_balance_of(options_contract)`): 

expected_custody =
    total_available             // User tradable cash balances
  + total_offer_collateral      // Maker CLOB offer collateral
  + total_bid_escrow            // Maker CLOB bid escrow
  + total_ticket_collateral     // CLOB peer-to-peer ticket collateral
  + total_ticket_premium        // Escrowed ticket premiums
  + total_pending_withdrawals   // In-flight async FT withdrawals
  + pool.equity                 // LP Vault equity (free + reserved)
  + pool.venue_locked           // Escrowed venue margin on open pool tickets
  + pool.venue                  // Realized venue fee revenue
  + pool.consolation            // Consolation bucket
Zero Insolvency by Construction: When a trader buys q contracts of a spread with width W paying P (split into LP notional P_lp and venue margin P_venue), the vault reserves max_payout - P_lp from free_equity. If free_equity cannot cover the net worst-case exposure, the transaction reverts before any state change.

5. Black-Scholes Bounded Spread Pricing Pricing Engine

Each vertical spread is priced analytically as the difference of two European options with strike-dependent skew implied volatility σ(K): 

// Skew Smile Volatility:
m = ln(S / K)
sigma(K) = max(0.05, sigma_atm * (1 + 0.35 * m))

// Black-Scholes European Call (r = 0):
d1 = (ln(S / K) + 0.5 * sigma^2 * T) / (sigma * sqrt(T))
d2 = d1 - sigma * sqrt(T)
C(S, K, T) = S * N(d1) - K * N(d2)

// Bounded Call Spread [L, H] Model Mid:
Mid_CallSpread(L, H) = clamp(C(S, L, T) - C(S, H, T), 0, H - L)

6. Bounded Put-Call Parity No-Arbitrage

On any bounded interval [L, H] with width W = H - L, a long Call Spread plus a long Put Spread over the same bounds pays exactly W at expiry regardless of where spot finishes: 

// Exact Bounded Spread Put-Call Parity (surface/src/pricing.rs):
Mid_PutSpread(L, H) = (H - L) - Mid_CallSpread(L, H)

This guarantees that an in-the-money Call Spread (where spot S > L) and an in-the-money Put Spread (where spot S < H) are always priced consistently with their intrinsic value, and closing an ITM position back to the pool always reflects its positive intrinsic gain.

USDT ($) Stablecoin Sizing & Fractional Contracts

All on-chain quantities and prices in `bounded_options` use a 6-decimal fixed-point scale (SCALE = 1,000,000). This means a trader does not have to buy integer contracts:

  • Size in USDT ($ Stablecoin): Enter any dollar budget (e.g., $10.00, $25.50, $100.00 USDT) and the protocol computes the exact fractional contract quantity q = USD_Budget / UnitBuyPrice down to 0.0001 contracts (100 on-chain micro-units).
  • Size in Contracts: Toggle to Contracts mode to specify any decimal or whole contract count (e.g., 0.25, 0.5, 1.5, or 10 contracts).

7. Fee & Margin Schedule (`core::fees`) Economics

There are no separate gas surcharges or hidden ticket fees. All venue compensation is embedded transparently inside the pool quote using integer ceiling/floor arithmetic in `crates/core/src/fees.rs`:

ActionFormulaVenue ParameterNotes
Standard Pool Buy (`Buy Price`) ceil(q × Mid × (1 + margin_bps / 10000)) +50 bps (0.50%) Includes the right to close early back to the LP Pool at any time before expiry.
Hold-to-Expiry Buy (`Hold Price`) ceil(q × Mid) 0 bps (0.00%) Zero venue fee (`Terms::FLAT`). Position is locked until expiry settlement.
Early Pool Close (`Sell Price`) floor(q × Mid × (1 - impact_bps / 10000)) -100 bps (1.00%) Instant liquidity back to the LP Pool prior to expiry; protected by your 1% slippage floor.
Expiry Settlement (`settle_ticket`) floor(q × Payoff(S_fixed)) 0 bps (0.00%) Full intrinsic payoff at the oracle committee fixed price, with zero settlement fee.

8. Pool Vault & LP Shares Liquidity

Liquidity providers deposit USDT into the Vault (`pool_deposit_from_balance` or `ft_transfer_call` with `msg: "pool-deposit"`) and receive proportional LP shares minted against the vault's current net asset value (`equity`).

  • Share Minting: If `shares == 0`, 1 share is minted per 1 USDT unit. Otherwise, minted_shares = floor(deposit × total_shares / equity).
  • Withdrawal Guard: An LP can redeem shares via `pool_withdraw(shares)` whenever the redeemed value floor(shares × equity / total_shares) ≤ free_equity. Capital backing active open tickets (`reserved`) remains locked until those tickets close or settle.

9. 4-Seat Oracle Committee & Settlement Consensus

Expiry settlement never trusts a single price feed or the venue's own operator alone. The `price_oracle` contract maintains an independent 4-seat signer committee:

  1. `kraken` — Independent Kraken spot signer process (`ed25519`).
  2. `okx` — Independent OKX spot signer process (`ed25519`).
  3. `pyth` — Independent Pyth Network price feed signer (`ed25519`).
  4. `venue-index` — The venue engine's median composite index (`ed25519`).
Quorum & Dispersion Rule: To fix an expired series (`request_settlement`), at least 2 fresh seats (`quorum = 2`) must agree within 30 bps (0.30%) dispersion tolerance. Once fixed, all expired positions automatically transition into Trade History with their exact settlement price, payout, and realized P&L.

11. Confidential Copy-Trading (`OutLayer SGX`) Strategies

Optionality supports non-custodial strategy replication where followers allocate a capped USDT budget and per-trade percentage to verified leaderboard traders:

  • Non-Custodial Mandates: Lead traders never custody follower funds. Followers can pause or revoke a mandate at any block.
  • Enclave Execution: Signal replication is sequenced inside OutLayer SGX enclave tasks so strategy orders are not exposed to public mempool front-running prior to settlement.
