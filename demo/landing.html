The Evolution of Options

From 1848 Grain Pits
to Autonomous Consensus

Before options were lines on glowing screens, they were legal promises written on paper to protect grain farmers from ruin. Optionality brings that core promise into verifiable onchain code.

1949 · The Pits

Open Outcry & Price Discovery

Stanley Kubrick photographed the Chicago Board of Trade trading floor in 1949. Thousands of traders screamed across hexagonal pits to establish fair value. Liquidity depended entirely on who was standing in the crowd.

1941 · The Origin

Harvest Uncertainty

Options began not as financial speculation, but as insurance for producers. A farmer lock-in a price floor so a bumper crop wouldn't collapse their livelihood before market day.

1848 · The Institution

LaSalle Street at Night

The Chicago Board of Trade standardized grain grading, warehouse receipts, and counterparty guarantees — the direct ancestors of modern clearinghouses.

1973 · The Modern Era

Standardized Listed Options

In April 1973, the CBOE opened in a former smoker's lounge. Call options were listed on 16 stocks. Black & Scholes published their pricing formula that same year.

Today · Optionality

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need
to know about Optionality.

From vertical spread payoff equations to pool solvency and oracle settlement rules. Clear answers directly from the technical documentation.

01 What instruments are traded on Optionality? +
Spreads

Optionality trades defined-risk vertical spreads (bull call spreads and bear put spreads) on ETH and BTC. Each instrument has two defined strikes [$K_L, K_H$] and a fixed expiry timestamp.

At expiry, a call spread pays clamp(S − K_L, 0, K_H − K_L) per contract, and a put spread pays clamp(K_H − S, 0, K_H − K_L). Payouts are fully cash-settled in USDT.

02 Can my position be liquidated or result in negative balance? +
Risk Model

No. Unlike linear perpetual futures, you can never be liquidated and will never owe margin. When buying a spread, the upfront premium paid is your absolute worst-case loss.

Even if the underlying cryptocurrency crashes 80% in an hour, you will never receive a margin call or face auto-deleveraging.

03 How is a winning option backed and guaranteed? +
Solvency

Every ticket is 100% solvent from the moment it is opened. When you trade against the liquidity pool, the smart contract calculates the maximum possible payout (contracts × spread_width) and locks that exact token collateral in reserve.

The pool cannot accept a trade unless it has sufficient unencumbered equity to guarantee the maximum potential winning payout.

04 How does expiry settlement work and who sets the price? +
Oracles

Settlement is governed by an independent 4-seat oracle committee: the venue's median index, Kraken, OKX, and Pyth Network. At expiry, signed price attestations are submitted onchain.

The contract requires at least two distinct fresh feeds to agree within tolerance. Once consensus is verified onchain, ticket payoffs are automatically unlockable.

05 Can I close my option position before expiry? +
Execution

Yes. Standard pool tickets can be closed early at any time at the pool's live exit quote (pool_close), with the early-close haircut already factored into the quote.

Traders who select the "Hold to Expiry" toggle enter at the pool's mid-market price with zero venue entry margin, agreeing to hold until settlement without early exit.

06 How do Liquidity Providers (LPs) earn in the vault? +
Yield

Liquidity providers deposit USDT to back the pool's worst-case collateral. In return, the pool collects the venue entry margin on every option written, plus any unexercised collateral from losing positions.

LPs receive yield organically from options market making and flow, without token emissions or inflationary subsidies.

07 Is this running on mainnet or testnet? +
Environment

The hosted venue at optionality.pro runs on an isolated NEAR sandbox with automated pool pricing and real price feeds from Binance, Bybit, and Coinbase.

You can top up test tokens freely in the app and test full options trading, hold-to-expiry zero-fee entry, and early closing against the LP vault without risking capital.
